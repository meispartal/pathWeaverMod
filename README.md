# PathWeaver

A client-side Minecraft fabric mod that weaves a glowing, permanent trail beneath your feet as you explore.

![PathWeaver Logo](logo.png)

## 🚀 Overview

**PathWeaver** is a client-side utility mod built for the Fabric toolchain. With a simple keypress, you can toggle a continuous, glowing cyan trail that tracks your movement perfectly. Whether you are mapping out paths between bases, navigating complex cave systems, or just want to leave a stylish mark on your world, PathWeaver has you covered.


## 🛠️ Configuration & Usage

1. **Activate/Deactivate:** Go to your game controls and bind the **Toggle PathWeaver** action to your preferred key.
2. **Weave your path:** Press the keybind once to begin generating the trail. Move anywhere you like—the smooth cyan line will follow.
3. **Stop Weaving:** Press the keybind again to stop adding new points to your current path.

## 📦 Supported Versions

PathWeaver is designed and optimized for modern Minecraft versions:

* **Fabric Loader:** Compatible with the latest stable releases.
